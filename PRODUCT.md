# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Clients looking for a portrait, lifestyle, family, group, animal, or gift-card photo shoot. They arrive from search or social links, scan the work, compare packages, then book a session.

## Product Purpose

Alex Photos is a showcase and booking site for Alexis Kodzaga, directeur artistique, photographe, and videaste. Success means visitors quickly understand the cinematic portrait direction, trust the portfolio, choose a package, and submit a reservation request.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, polished, intimate. The interface should feel like a low-light photo set: dark space, controlled gold light, measured motion, and strong image presence.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates, cheap glitter effects, stock agency layouts, loud neon gradients, and decorative UI that competes with the photographs.

## Design Principles

Show the work first. Let photography carry credibility before copy does.

Use gold like light, not paint. Gold accents should feel precise, warm, and premium.

Keep booking obvious. Reservation paths should stay visible without interrupting the mood.

Make motion quiet. Animation should feel like a camera reveal, with reduced-motion support.

Preserve contrast. Dark surfaces and fine typography must remain readable on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default target: WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Interactive controls need clear focus, readable labels, and comfortable tap targets.
